AI is allowed in this repository provided the following rules are followed:

1. All commit messages, documentation, comments, and discussion are authored by a human. You are
   responsible for the output of the code and should be able to discuss the changes made should
   something need to be changed or discussed
2. It should be hard to clock that it's an AI PR. You are still held to the rigor and standard of a
   human working a professional job. This means PRs that are drive by "Bug fixes" without prior
   discussion, fixups not relevant to the changes proposed, or other markers could lead to the PR
   being rejected or asked to be reworked
3. "I don't know I just asked Claude to fix it" is not a good reason. Again you need to know what
   you are creating

This policy is subject to change and the current version will be available in this file at the tip
of main.
